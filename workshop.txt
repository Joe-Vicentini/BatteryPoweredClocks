title=Clocks Have Batteries
description=[h1]Clocks Have Batteries[/h1]
description=Wall clocks shouldn't run forever without power. This mod gives Project Zomboid's wall clocks batteries that slowly drain, can be removed and replaced, and determine whether a clock keeps time or stops.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Individual batteries:[/b] Each supported wall clock starts with its own randomly determined battery charge. Old, previously unvisited clocks can already have drained batteries.
description=[*][b]Slow battery drain:[/b] Batteries gradually lose charge as in-game time passes, even when the clock's area is unloaded.
description=[*][b]Stopped clocks:[/b] Removing a battery or letting it run flat stops that clock's simulated time at the moment it loses power.
description=[*][b]Replaceable batteries:[/b] Remove a battery and keep its remaining charge, or insert a specific battery from your inventory or carried bags.
description=[*][b]Time can become inaccurate:[/b] When power returns without setting the time, the clock resumes from where it stopped instead of magically knowing the correct time.
description=[*][b]Set the time with a watch:[/b] Carry a watch (equipped or unequipped) to set an out-of-sync running clock. If the sandbox option is enabled, inserting a battery while carrying a watch also sets the time automatically. Simply finding a watch will not synchronize nearby clocks.
description=[*][b]Moving clocks:[/b] Picking up a clock returns its installed battery to your inventory. Placing that clock again leaves it without a battery, preserving its stopped time until you power it.
description=[*][b]Disassembling clocks:[/b] Successfully dismantling a wall clock returns its installed battery, if it has one.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How to use[/h2]
description=Right-click a supported wall clock when you are nearby:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Remove clock battery[/b] shows the installed battery's remaining charge and lets you take it.
description=[*][b]Insert clock battery[/b] lets you choose from the batteries you are carrying, with their charges shown.
description=[*][b]Set clock time[/b] is available when the clock is running, its time is incorrect, and you have a watch in your inventory.
description=[/list]
description=Battery interactions take time, like changing a battery in another device, and your character moves into reach before performing the action.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Customize the behavior under [b]Clocks Have Batteries[/b] in the Sandbox Options menu:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Battery drain:[/b] Percentage points lost per in-game day (default: 0.5).
description=[*][b]Minimum initial charge:[/b] Lowest possible randomly rolled starting charge (default: 20%).
description=[*][b]Age unvisited clocks:[/b] Account for elapsed in-game time when first discovering a clock (enabled by default).
description=[*][b]Automatically set time when inserting a battery:[/b] Synchronize the clock on insertion if the character carries a watch (enabled by default).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility and limitations[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Made for [b]Project Zomboid Build 42[/b]; not intended for Build 41.
description=[*]Currently implemented for [b]single-player[/b]. Multiplayer support is not implemented.
description=[*]Supports known vanilla wall-clock sprites, including school-style and square wall clocks. Clocks added by other mods may not be recognized.
description=[*]Clock battery state and stopped time persist in saves. Animated clock-hand coverage on stopped clocks depends on Build 42's rendering and has not been fully verified in-game.
description=[/list]
